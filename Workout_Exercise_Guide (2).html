Warm-up 3–5 minutes before every strength session

Do this, in order

  1. 1–2 min easy walk on the pad or march in place.
  2. 10 arm circles each direction, then 10 band-free "pull-aparts" (arms out, squeeze shoulder blades).
  3. 10 bodyweight squats, slow, as deep as is comfortable.
  4. 10 hip hinges: hands on hips, push hips back with a flat back, stand up tall.
  5. 10 glute bridges (on lower days) or 10 wall push-ups (on upper days).
  6. One light set of your first exercise with half the weight, about 6–8 reps.

Universal form rules

  • Brace: before each rep, take a breath and tighten your stomach as if someone were about to poke it.
  • Neutral spine: keep your back flat and your neck in line with it. Look at the floor a little ahead of you when bent over.
  • Control the lowering: take 2–3 seconds to lower and about 1 second to lift.
  • Breathe: exhale on the hard part, inhale on the way back.
  • Pain rule: muscle burn is fine. Stop if you feel sharp or joint pain, and check the demo again.

Progression when and how to make it harder

Double-progression rule

  1. Pick a weight you can lift for the bottom of the rep range with clean form.
  2. Each session, try to add a rep or two.
  3. When both sets hit the top of the range (e.g. 2 × 12), move up by your smallest plate increment next time.
  4. Reps drop back to the bottom of the range. Build up again.
